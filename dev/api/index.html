GameTracing.GameTracing — Module
GameTracing

Homotopy continuation for logit quantal response equilibria. trace_endpoint follows the branch of a logit game from zero precision (infinite temperature), where the only equilibrium is the flat one, up to a target precision with BifurcationKit's pseudo-arclength continuation (PALC), across folds. logit_solve and its kin run on BifurcationKit (BifurcationKitSettings) or on PathFollowing (PathFollowingSettings). Failures are values (TraceFailure, its cause a Moshi sum TraceCause), never exceptions.

GameTracing.BifurcationKitSettings — Type
BifurcationKitSettings{E}(; max_step, aggressiveness, step_metric,
                 corrector, max_steps, tangent, bordered_solver, step_control, orientation_check, palc_corrector,
                 palc_options, continuation_options)

BifurcationKit PALC controls for trace_endpoint, in (w, λ) with w = logits / λ the payoff gaps and λ the precision. Step sizes are arclength in step_metric's norm (StepDistance). The defaults are gambit_settings(E, GAMBIT_MAX_ACCEL): Gambit's growth limit. The first step comes from the game (opening_step).

  • max_step: the largest arclength step, which also caps the first. A step whose corrector fails halves; the trace ends as TraceStuck once its predictor moves the point no more than a few ulps of its size (step_unresolved). BifurcationKit's dsmin is zero: its halving stops at a zero step. After the predictor stops moving, BifurcationKit still halves to zero through the subnormals, about 150 halvings of one refused residual evaluation each.
  • aggressiveness (BifurcationKit's a): without a step_control growth limit, a step grows by 1 + a ((N - n) / N)² after a corrector of n of its N = corrector.newton_iterations iterations. The default is GAMBIT_MAX_ACCEL - 1, the growth limit's largest growth.
  • corrector: a CorrectorSettings.
  • step_metric: a TraceGeometry, the norm a trace steps in and its state weight. The default is default_step_metric, both engines' default. The first tangent is the exact tangent at the flat start (opening_tangent).
  • max_steps: continuation steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence (MaxSteps), or nothing (the default) for no limit. A trace then ends at its target or where it cannot go on: an unresolved step, an unmoved point, a stall at rounding noise or a broken solve.
  • tangent: BifurcationKit's tangent algorithm, passed to PALC as is. Bordered() (the default) solves the bordered Jacobian system. Secant() extrapolates the last two points and saves that solve. Do not use it where a small payoff perturbation unfolds a pitchfork: there it crosses onto another arm (the test "a pitchfork unfolded by a payoff perturbation"). Over 40 random unfoldings at payoff scale 1e-4 it kept the branch in none, Bordered() in all 40.
  • bordered_solver: a BorderedSolver.
  • step_control: a StepControl, or nothing for none.
  • orientation_check orients a bordered tangent by the determinant of the bordered matrix. Only MatrixSolve has a determinant, so BorderingSolve needs orientation_check = false; the constructor throws otherwise.
  • palc_corrector: PALC's corrector keyword, nothing by default, which is BifurcationKit's own Newton method (newton_palc; it stops on leaving [0, P], P the target precision). BifurcationKit.FreeParameterNewton() leaves λ free there instead, the trace's choice before this field. CorrectorReach reads any corrector's Newton callbacks.
  • palc_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.PALC. A keyword the trace sets itself (tangent, θ, dotθ, bls, step_control, orientation_check, corrector, orientation_dot) throws an ArgumentError at the trace: the step shrinking, tangent orientation and geometry rest on them. Set them through the fields above.
  • continuation_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.ContinuationPar. A keyword the trace sets itself (continuation_options lists them) throws the same way: CorrectorReach needs tol = 0, and the trace ends at p_max.

GameTracing.CorrectorDistance — Type
CorrectorDistance{E}(; tolerance, settle_ulps, target_correction)

How the corrector measures a move between two points [w; λ]: Gambit's norm (path.cc), the root sum of squares of every strategy's change in log probability, past each strategy's settle_ulps ulps of its rounding scale (LogProbabilityChange). A move within rounding counts zero, in the settle test, step control, the split check and the reach alike. The corrector settles once a correction moves the profile no more than tolerance (LogProbabilitySettled). Step control's target first correction is target_correction, or nothing to drop the term. A set target must lie above tolerance (settle_tolerance). Where a strategy leaves the support its log probability falls like -λ, and its corrections hold the steps short, as in Gambit.

GameTracing.CorrectorSettings — Type
CorrectorSettings{E}(; reach, max_kl, logit_tolerance, settle_ulps, pin_tolerance, contraction_limit,
                     contraction_offset, noise_ratio, noise_limit, newton_iterations)

The corrector's controls (CorrectorReach); nothing switches a limit off. Every move is Gambit's norm, the root sum of squares of the log probabilities' change (LogProbabilityChange). reach: a corrector that moves the profile further from the predictor than this times the predictor's own move is refused. shift_share: the share of that reach a redraw's predicted root shift may use (RootShift). max_kl: a corrected point that moves any player's mixed strategy further from the predictor's than this KL is refused (largest_kl); the game's pass must define logit_ranges. A step's turn is limited by StepControl's turn_limit.

Convergence is Gambit's test (path.cc, c_tol 1e-4 on the Euclidean norm in log probabilities): a Newton correction that moves the profile at most logit_tolerance, at a precision within the relative pin_tolerance of its pin. Each strategy's change first loses settle_ulps ulps of its own rounding scale (log_probability_corrections). A Float32 logit near 1e3 rounds by 1.2e-4, past the default tolerance, so without this allowance a Float32 trace stalls near λ 300 on 20x20 games. A correction that moves the profile more than contraction_limit times the one before it (plus logit_tolerance times contraction_offset) fails to contract; BifurcationKit's step growth reads the same rate (corrector_quality). Suspected noise is confirmed when the halved step's stalled correction is at least noise_ratio of the suspect's (corrected!). Halving keeps noise's corrections alike (ratio 1); it halves those of a predictor off the branch's tangent (ratio 1/2) and quarters those of its curvature (1/4). The default, 1/√2, splits 1/2 from 1. A stall counts as noise only within noise_limit times the noise floor (noise_floor): the larger of logit_tolerance and the largest settled correction so far. A corrector past it is far from any root, so the step halves at once. newton_iterations: Newton iterations per corrector (BifurcationKit's default, 25; Gambit's is 100).

GameTracing.LogitGame — Type
LogitGame(pass!!, target_precision)

A logit game whose equilibria solve z = λ G(z, λ) at precision λ = 1 / temperature, G the payoff gains the logits scale. pass!!(gain, gain_slope, gain_precision_slope, z, λ) writes G(z, λ), its Jacobian ∂G/∂z and its slope ∂G/∂λ into the three arrays, from one evaluation, and returns them (into!!): mutable arrays changed in place, static arrays new. The mutable arrays are views into the trace's own residual and Jacobian (branch_rows!!), so a pass allocates nothing of its own. A pass that needs work arrays of its own gets them from pass_workspace. G must be finite at λ = 0, where the flat logits are the only equilibrium. target_precision is the precision a trace ends at, non-negative and finite (ArgumentError otherwise). A traced pass also defines logit_ranges and gap_bound.

LogitQREProfile(gains, lam, logits)

One point of a normal-form game's logit QRE branch, as pygambit's LogitQREMixedStrategyProfile: the precision lam (Gambit's λ, π ∝ exp(λ u), the trace's precision) and profile, each player's mixed strategy (mixed_strategies). logits are the trace's log odds against each player's first strategy; they stay finite where a probability underflows.

GameTracing.NormalFormGains — Type
NormalFormGains(payoffs)

The pass!! of a normal-form game with any number of strategies per player. payoffs[p][s] is player p's payoff at the pure profile s; every array has one type and one size, each player's strategy count along its own axis. Player p's logits are z_pk = log(π_pk / π_p1) for its strategies k ≥ 2, against its first strategy, stacked player by player (logit_ranges). Its gains are G_pk = U_p(k, π) - U_p(1, π), its expected payoffs against the rivals' mixed strategies, which do not depend on the precision. A pass writes into work arrays from pass_workspace; called without them, it builds its own. Static payoffs (SArray) and logits (SVector) take static work arrays, so a pass is isbits and runs in a GPU kernel; the arithmetic is the mutable pass's, step for step.

GameTracing.PathFollowingSettings — Type
PathFollowingSettings{E}(; max_step, max_steps, distance, newton_iterations, contraction_limit, target_contraction,
                         target_turn, max_step_growth, reach, orientation_check, split_check, max_refusals,
                         min_step_ulps, step_metric, linear_solver, endpoint_tolerance, endpoint_ulps)

The controls of a trace on PathFollowing's pseudo-arclength continuation, an EngineSettings for logit_solve and trace_endpoint. The trace runs in [w; λ], the payoff gaps w = z / λ and the precision λ.

  • step_metric: a TraceGeometry, the norm of steps and turns in [w; λ] (trace_metric) at target precision one, frozen at each accepted point. The default, default_step_metric, is gambit_settings' too.
  • max_step: the largest step in that norm; Inf sets none. The first step comes from the game (opening_step).
  • max_steps: accepted steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence (MaxSteps), or nothing (the default) for no limit. Each accepted step moves the point past its rounding (min_step_ulps), and a step that cannot fails.
  • distance: a CorrectorDistance, how the corrector measures a move: Gambit's norm in log probabilities. It carries the corrector's settle tolerance and step control's target correction.
  • newton_iterations, contraction_limit: PathFollowing's Newton, its corrections under distance. It settles on the last correction, as Gambit does. The contraction limit must lie in (0, 1/2].
  • target_contraction, target_turn, max_step_growth, reach: PathFollowing's StepControl; nothing drops a target. The turn target is in radians under step_metric. A converged point farther than reach step lengths from its predictor refuses the step, as one that jumped to another branch, unless distance puts the move within the corrector's settle tolerance. Inf switches the refusal off.
  • orientation_check: PathFollowing's orientation check. DeterminantSign refuses every step where sign det([J; tᵀ]) changes: the step ran back along the branch or crossed a bifurcation point. nothing switches the check off.
  • split_check: PathFollowing's HalfStepCheck{E}, or nothing to switch it off. A step with factor at least trigger runs again in two half steps, which must reach its point within tolerance in distance. The default trigger is 1.3 (default_split_check), and the tolerance 10 times distance's settle tolerance. A zero settle tolerance has no default: pass split_check. Without the check, at the default step control, 4 of 20,000 random two-action games and 3 of 1200 unfolded pitchforks left the principal branch on a step that passed every other test. Tolerance evidence, at growth 2 and the summed metric, under the former Fisher–Rao distance in Float32 and Float64. A sweep with the reach on and off: 203 seeds of 20 two-action games, and battle of the sexes plus 1e-4 to 1e-1 randn payoffs, 300 draws per scale. The half steps flagged all 22 jumps and 148 of 196,594 steps that kept the branch (0.075%). Kept steps reached their point within 2.5e-5 at the 99.99th percentile. Each jump failed or landed at least 0.27 away. So that distance's 5e-4 tolerance lay 20 times above that percentile and 550 times under the nearest jump. At the default step control, trigger 1.3 refused 21 of 565,078 kept steps in the hunt (0.004%).
  • max_refusals: refused attempts in one step before the trace fails as NoConvergence (MaxRefusals), or nothing (the default) for no limit. Each refusal shrinks the step, so a step that never converges fails TraceStuck (UnresolvedStep) at the float type's resolution.
  • min_step_ulps: a predictor that moves no coordinate more than this many ulps fails the trace as TraceStuck (UnresolvedStep).
  • linear_solver: the LinearSolve.jl algorithm for the bordered systems.
  • endpoint_tolerance, endpoint_ulps: trace_endpoint's last Newton correction at the target must move the profile no more than endpoint_tolerance in Gambit's norm, past endpoint_ulps ulps of each strategy's rounding scale (LogProbabilitySettled, verified_landing). The defaults are BifurcationKit's logit_tolerance and settle_ulps, so both engines bound Traced.logit_accuracy alike.

GameTracing.PrecisionTerm — Type

The precision's term in FisherDistance: TargetPrecisionTerm (Δλ / P)², or LogPrecisionTerm (Δλ / (1 + λ))², the step in log(1 + λ) to first order, LogitNash's path variable. The log term does not depend on P, so the target only caps the trace. The plain distances weigh the precision at the target alone.

source
GameTracing.StepControl — Type
StepControl{E}(; distance_limit, max_step_growth, turn_limit)

BifurcationKit's CorrectorQuality thresholds, which shrink a step whose corrector looks poor. distance_limit is the Euclidean length of a Newton step's logit part (Gambit's 0.4 in log probabilities); max_step_growth bounds a step's growth (Gambit's max_accel 1.1), and the step then shrinks as the distance or contraction nears its limit. turn_limit refuses a step whose tangent turns this many radians from the last one, Allgower and Georg's test (6.1.10) for a step across a fold onto another branch; 1 is under Gambit's quarter turn (t·newT < 0, path.cc). Without BifurcationKitSettings' orientation_check each tangent keeps the last one's direction, so a turn α reads min(α, π - α): the test catches turns near a right angle and misses a reversal. The corrector's contraction rate (CorrectorSettings) also slows growth. nothing switches a threshold off.

source
GameTracing.StepDistance — Type

The trace's step length in a state x = [w; λ] of k payoff gaps w at target precision P, at state_weight (the gaps' weight). Gaps and precision enter in the units P w and λ / P, so each plain distance weighs Δwᵢ² by P² and Δλ² by 1 / P²:

  • AveragedDistance: state_weight P² ‖Δw‖² / k + (1 - state_weight) (Δλ / P)².
  • SummedDistance: state_weight P² ‖Δw‖² + (Δλ / P)².
  • FisherDistance: state_weight Σₚ Δzₚᵀ Gₚ Δzₚ plus a PrecisionTerm: the Fisher information of the strategy profile at the logits z = λ w, pulled back to the state through Δz = λ Δw + w Δλ. Player p's block Gₚ = diag(qₚ) - qₚqₚᵀ spans its logits (logit_ranges); FisherMetric applies it.

The Fisher distance freezes G, w and λ at each accepted point. The trace's plain products (trace_dot) measure it as SummedDistance. The corrector's reach check measures in Gambit's norm under every distance (within_reach).

TraceGeometry{E}(; distance, state_weight, precision_term)

A plain distance takes TargetPrecisionTerm only; another throws an ArgumentError. The default is both engines' (default_step_metric): the Fisher distance at state_weight = 0.1 with LogPrecisionTerm. GameTracing.logit_solve_branch — Method
logit_solve_branch(gains; maxregret, max_accel, stall_window, settings)

pygambit's logit_solve_branch: every accepted point of the principal branch of the normal-form game gains, from the first step to the first point where no player's regret passes maxregret times the payoff range. As in Gambit, no set λ cap ends the trace. Its target is precision_ceiling, which only makes the target finite. For a set precision, use logit_solve_lambda. The trace fails PrecisionExhausted (AtEndpoint) where the regret stalls at its rounding floor for stall_window points (regret_stalled, DEFAULT_STALL_WINDOW), or at its target unsettled. There, a failed endpoint correction gives its own cause instead (regret_stall). Any other failure of the trace is returned as is. A failure's context holds maxregret in the trace's element type E (RegretContext). maxregret defaults to default_maxregret(E). max_accel and settings are logit_solve_lambda's.

pygambit's logit_solve_lambda: the LogitQREProfile on the principal branch of the normal-form game gains (NormalFormGains) at each precision in lam, a number or a vector, in lam's order, or the first trace's failure. max_accel caps the trace's step growth (gambit_settings); settings, an EngineSettings, replaces it. pygambit's first_step has no counterpart: every trace's first step comes from the game (opening_step). GameTracing.logit_solve_lambda_batch — Method
logit_solve_lambda_batch(games, lam; settings, backend, workgroupsize)

logit_solve_lambda on PathFollowing for each of games at the one precision lam: a ProfileResult per game, in order, on the host. Each game traces in its own work item on the KernelAbstractions backend (PathFollowing's land_batch), from its flat start with its own first step (opening_step). A failed trace gives the TraceFailure that logit_solve_lambda gives, its context lam (LambdaContext).

  • games: NormalFormGains of one concrete type with static payoffs (SArray), every player with the same strategy count.
  • settings: PathFollowingSettings in the payoffs' float type, with linear_solver = PathFollowing.StaticLU().
  • backend: the device that runs the traces. The problems copy to it, and the landings copy back.
  • workgroupsize: the work items per group. The CPU backend runs each group on one thread, so use a small group there.

lam must be non-negative and finite in the trace's element type (ArgumentError otherwise). The traces run on static arrays and StaticLU, whose arithmetic differs from the mutable trace's: the profiles agree with logit_solve_lambda's to the corrector's tolerance, not to the bit.

GameTracing.pass_workspace — Method
pass_workspace(pass!!, logits)

The work arrays pass!! needs beyond its outputs, in the element type, length and storage of logits, or nothing (the default) when it needs none. A pass with work arrays is called as pass!!(work, gain, gain_slope, gain_precision_slope, z, λ) and returns the work first, then its outputs (into!!). A trace builds them once per game and shares them across every evaluation of that game. So a pass may read what pass_workspace wrote, such as the game's constants, and writes every other work array before it reads it. The work belongs to its game: no other game's pass gets it. Each trace builds its own, so a game shared by two traces stays safe. A redrawn game's work must have the type of the trace's (swapped). Static work arrays come back new from each pass, and a static trace drops them: the next pass starts again from pass_workspace's.

GameTracing.trace_endpoint — Method
trace_endpoint(game, width, settings; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw, context)

Follow game's branch of width logits from the flat start (zero logits, the only root at precision zero) to game.target_precision on the engine of settings (engine_endpoint). Return Ok(Traced) logits there or Err(TraceFailure) for the failure that stopped short (a TraceResult).

keep_going(logits, λ) runs at each accepted point before the next step's corrector, and false stops the trace. accept_corrected(logits, λ, converged) runs as each corrector ends, and false refuses a converged point, which shrinks the step. redraw(logits, λ, shift) swaps the game mid-trace; only BifurcationKitSettings runs it (check_redraw). context, a NamedTuple, rides on every failure as its own field (TraceFailure).

A non-finite start fails NotFinite (AtStart), and a zero target returns the flat start with no step. A trace that stops short fails NoConvergence (MaxSteps) when it spends a set settings.max_steps, and TraceStuck, Singular or PrecisionExhausted otherwise (TraceCause). The point at the target passes one more Newton correction there, settled in Gambit's norm, or fails.

source

Moshi keeps the docstring of each failure sum in its own module. This block lists them.

GameTracing.TraceCause — Module

Why a trace stopped short of its target precision, a Moshi sum in the trace's element type E, one variant per cause. A variant with no field of type E takes it explicitly: TraceCause.NotFinite{E}(AtStart).

  • NoConvergence(limit): the trace spent an opt-in budget, its StopLimit.
  • TraceStuck(reason): the trace stuck for its StuckReason.
  • NotFinite(stage): the game was not finite AtStart or AtEndpoint (TraceStage).
  • Singular(stage): a linear solve broke AtStart, MidTrace or AtEndpoint.
  • PrecisionExhausted(stage, stall): Newton's corrections stopped contracting at the float type's rounding noise (StalledCorrection), MidTrace (CorrectorReach) or AtEndpoint; a wider float type goes further. logit_solve also fails AtEndpoint where the regret stops falling at its rounding floor (regret_stalled). Its stall holds the endpoint check's correction at the last point (regret_stall).

GameTracing.EngineRecord — Module

The trace engine's own record where a trace failed, a Moshi sum in the trace's element type E. A variant with no field of type E takes it explicitly: EngineRecord.NoRecord{E}().

  • NoRecord(): no engine record, as no engine ran (a non-finite start), or GameTracing's own check ended the trace: a regret stall, a logit_solve trace at precision_ceiling unsettled, or a check of a PathFollowing trace.
  • BifurcationKitTrace(accepted_step, precision_min, precision_max, logit_tangent, gap_tangent, tangent_precision, logit_accuracy, message): the arclength step of the last accepted point (zero when none was accepted), the precision's range, the tangent there in logits (logit_tangent, dz = λ dw + w dλ) and in payoff gaps (gap_tangent, tangent_precision), the largest last correction in Gambit's norm of an accepted point, and the caught exception's showerror text (empty when none ended the trace).
  • PathFollowingStep(failure): the PathFollowing.StepFailure that ended the trace.
