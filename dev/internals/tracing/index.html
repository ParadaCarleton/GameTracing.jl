GameTracing.engine_endpoint — Method
engine_endpoint(game, start, pass_work, settings::BifurcationKitSettings, callbacks, context)

trace_endpoint on BifurcationKit from the finite start = [w; 0] (trace_start) on game's pass work pass_work, to a nonzero target, with callbacks = (; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw). A refusal by accept_corrected halves the step. A stop short of the target fails TraceStuck for a step below the float type's resolution, an unchanged accepted point or a refusal, and Singular for a broken linear solve. The endpoint passes verified_endpoint.

redraw(logits, λ, shift) runs at each accepted point short of the target precision that keep_going passes, and returns nothing, a new game of the same type and target precision, or the ShiftedGame that shift(game) (RootShift) returned for one. That check tells whether a candidate game's root lies within the corrector's reach, so a caller can refine a draw that does not before it returns; the restart reuses its factorization of the new game's Jacobian. A new game replaces the map for the steps after that point: the trace restarts there along the new map's tangent, oriented by the old one, with the step it held, and its next corrector lands on the new map under the same step control. The endpoint, with no step after it, is never redrawn.

GameTracing.verified_endpoint — Method
verified_endpoint(progress, outcome, game, pass_work, corrector, context)

The TraceResult at progress, the last accepted point, on the target. One more iteration of the corrector that landed it checks it. BifurcationKit corrects a step past p_max by Newton's method with λ pinned at the bound (_corrector_at_bound), so the check is that step: a dense LU solve of game's branch Jacobian on its pass work pass_work, as DefaultLS. That corrector can stop on a lucky draw of evaluation noise, a correction under the tolerance by chance (issue #134).

The correction is pinned_correction's, which fails NotFinite or Singular. An unsettled correction (as settled: past corrector.logit_tolerance beyond each strategy's settle_ulps, log_probability_corrections) fails PrecisionExhausted (AtEndpoint). Its StalledCorrection runs from the endpoint (gaps, precision) to a plain Newton iterate pinned at the endpoint's precision, arclength zero. Else the point is Ok(Traced), its logit_accuracy at least the correction.

GameTracing.CorrectorReach — Type
CorrectorReach(accept_corrected, settings, geometry, target_precision, start, predicted, step_length, ranges)

The trace's BifurcationKit callback_newton, which also decides the corrector's convergence: a settled iterate ends Newton's method converged (NewtonConverged), and a correction that fails to contract stops it (corrected!). Two such corrections in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move and within noise_limit of the noise floor (noise_floor), are suspected rounding noise (noise), confirmed at the next halved step; the check then refuses every corrector (noise_confirmed). A non-finite residual stops the corrector, and so does any iterate whose profile is further from the predictor's than settings.reach times the predictor's own move (within_reach); the step then halves. A predictor within RESOLUTION_ULPS ulps of each of the point's coordinates (step_unresolved) refuses that corrector and every later one (unresolved): no smaller step can add a point, so the trace ends as TraceStuck, or as PrecisionExhausted when the corrector before it stopped past its reach (unresolved_noise). On a smooth branch that distance is the branch's curvature times ds² / 2, so halving brings any step under it, while an iterate that lands on another branch is far from it. A converged closing point is refused the same way past reach, and past a player's KL KL(corrected ‖ predicted) of settings.max_kl over the players of ranges (largest_kl). Every move reads ranges (logit_ranges). A predictor that did not move has no reach to compare. accept_corrected(logits, λ, converged) then sees every other closing point. predicted holds the current step's predictor, iterate the current solve's last iterate and stalled the iterate of the last stall or reach failure on record (noise, reach_failure, record_stall!), all updated in place, mutable copies of predicted and start in similar(predicted); state (CorrectorState) holds the scalars that change between calls. geometry measures the step length at target_precision.

GameTracing.TraceFinalise — Type
TraceFinalise(keep_going, redraw, slot, settings)

BifurcationKit's finalise_solution of one trace segment. An accepted point bitwise equal to the branch's last one (a step below the point's rounding) stops the trace, a stall. The trace goes on while keep_going(logits, precision) holds. Then redraw(logits, precision, shift) may return a new game, where shift is the point's RootShift check under settings: slot, a Ref to the current RedrawSlot (the constructor wraps the one it is given), takes it and the segment stops, for trace_endpoint to correct the point onto it and restart. A redraw that returns nothing or the current game swaps nothing. JULIA_DEBUG=trace lists every point.

GameTracing.corrected! — Method
corrected!(check, iterate, proceed)

check's verdict on a Newton iterate that its other tests let proceed. The first iterate is the solve's start and never settles. Each later one is a correction from the iterate before, in Gambit's norm: it settles (NewtonConverged) when settled by its part past each strategy's settle_ulps (log_probability_corrections). A correction past contraction_limit times the one before (plus logit_tolerance · contraction_offset) fails to contract, Gambit's test (path.cc), and stops the corrector, so the step halves. Two in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move and within noise_limit of the noise floor (noise_floor), are suspected noise (noise), the iterate in check.stalled; the step still halves. A second such solve before any settles, its correction at least noise_ratio of the suspect's, confirms it (noise_confirmed): halving shrinks the corrections of a predictor past a fold, not noise. Every later solve refuses.

GameTracing.settled — Method

Whether check's corrector has settled iterate, reached by a Newton step whose move past each strategy's settle_ulps is correction (log_probability_corrections): the step moved the profile no more than logit_tolerance past its rounding, and the precision λ is at its pin to the relative pin_tolerance. GameTracing.step_unresolved — Method

Whether the predictor predicted moves no coordinate of start by more than RESOLUTION_ULPS · eps(E) of its own size: the ODE solvers' per-coordinate error test, |Δz_i| ≤ abstol + reltol |z_i|, with abstol zero. Coordinates of different scales (payoff gaps near 1, precision up to 1e6) each count, and any move of a zero coordinate resolves.

GameTracing.RootShift — Type
RootShift(point, border, limit)
RootShift(point, step, border, settings, ranges)

The check a redraw hook receives at the accepted point point = [w; λ]. Called on a candidate game, it returns the ShiftedGame: one evaluation of the game's map and Jacobian at point, the bordered system under the corrector's border factored once, and the root shift δ from [∂F/∂x; borderᵀ] δ = [-F(point); 0], one Newton step of the bordered corrector. The old map's residual at an accepted point is within the corrector's tolerance, so -F(point) stands for F_old(point) - F_new(point). The shift is within when its move is at most limit: settings.shift_share of the corrector's reach on the next step, the predictor step (CorrectorSettings) over the players of ranges (logit_ranges), or nothing when the reach is off, and every game passes. A hook returns the ShiftedGame to install its game, and the restart reuses its factorization.

source
GameTracing.ShiftedGame — Type
ShiftedGame(game, point, factorization, shift, shift_move, within, pass_work)

A candidate game at the accepted point point = [w; λ] (RootShift): its bordered Jacobian [∂F/∂x; bᵀ] there, factored, its root shift δ, that shift's move shift_move (LogProbabilityChange), whether the move is within the check's limit, and the game's pass work arrays (pass_workspace), which a swap hands on to the trace. A non-finite map or Jacobian at point has no factorization (nothing), a NaN shift and is never within; a restart on it fails as LAPACK's check of a non-finite matrix does, at the swap point.

GameTracing.swapped — Method
swapped(slot, check, redrawn)

The RedrawSlot after a redraw hook's result redrawn, slot itself unless it swaps the game: nothing and the current game do not; a new game swaps as the ShiftedGame that check (RootShift) builds for it, and a ShiftedGame the hook returned must come from check. A swap sets pending, the factorization and the root shift the restart reads; a shift beyond the corrector's reach (not within) is dropped, so a root out of reach fails stuck at the swap instead of jumping branches. A game of another type, or with another target precision, is a caller error: the trace's precision would change meaning. So is pass work (pass_workspace) of another type: the trace holds one type of work.

source
GameTracing.projection_step — Method
projection_step(game, logits, precision, geometry)

One minimum-norm Gauss–Newton step from x = [logits / λ; λ] onto game's branch F(x) = 0 in payoff gaps, in geometry's plain norm W at the game's target precision (plain_weights): dx = -W⁻¹ Jᵀ (J W⁻¹ Jᵀ)⁻¹ F. It is the minimum-norm solution of (J W^-½) y = -F by pivoted QR, scaled back by W^-½; the normal matrix is never formed. The step moves λ as well, so it stays defined at a fold, where a correction at fixed λ is singular. Payoff gaps w = z / λ exist at every finite λ ≠ 0, so λ must be one.
