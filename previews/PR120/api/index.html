GameTracing.GameTracing — Module
GameTracing

Homotopy continuation for logit quantal response equilibria. trace_endpoint follows the branch of a logit game from zero precision (infinite temperature), where the only equilibrium is the flat one, up to a target precision with BifurcationKit's pseudo-arclength continuation (PALC), across folds. logit_solve and its kin run on BifurcationKit (BifurcationKitSettings) or on PathFollowing (PathFollowingSettings). Failures are values (TraceFailure), never exceptions.

GameTracing.BifurcationKitSettings — Type
BifurcationKitSettings{E}(; first_step, max_step, aggressiveness, step_metric,
                 corrector, max_steps, tangent, bordered_solver, step_control, orientation_check, palc_corrector,
                 palc_options, continuation_options)

BifurcationKit PALC controls for trace_endpoint, in (w, λ) with w = logits / λ the payoff gaps and λ the precision. Step sizes are arclength in step_metric's norm (StepDistance); the default is the Fisher distance (TraceGeometry).

  • first_step, max_step: first and largest arclength step. A step whose corrector fails halves; the trace ends as TraceStuck once its predictor moves the point no more than a few ulps of its size (step_unresolved). BifurcationKit's dsmin is zero: its halving stops at a zero step. After the predictor stops moving, BifurcationKit still halves to zero through the subnormals, about 150 halvings of one refused residual evaluation each.
  • aggressiveness (BifurcationKit's a): without a step_control growth limit, a step grows by 1 + a ((N - n) / N)² after a corrector of n of its N = corrector.newton_iterations iterations.
  • corrector: a CorrectorSettings.
  • step_metric: a TraceGeometry, the norm a trace steps in and its state weight. The first tangent is the exact tangent at the flat start (opening_tangent).
  • max_steps: continuation steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence.
  • tangent: BifurcationKit's tangent algorithm, passed to PALC as is. Bordered() (the default) solves the bordered Jacobian system. Secant() extrapolates the last two points and saves that solve. Do not use it where a small payoff perturbation unfolds a pitchfork: there it crosses onto another arm (the test "a pitchfork unfolded by a payoff perturbation"). Over 40 random unfoldings at payoff scale 1e-4 it kept the branch in none, Bordered() in all 40.
  • bordered_solver: a BorderedSolver.
  • step_control: a StepControl, or nothing for none.
  • orientation_check orients a bordered tangent by the determinant of the bordered matrix. Only MatrixSolve has a determinant, so BorderingSolve needs orientation_check = false; the constructor throws otherwise.
  • palc_corrector: PALC's corrector keyword, nothing by default, which is BifurcationKit's own Newton method (newton_palc; it stops on leaving [0, P], P the target precision). BifurcationKit.FreeParameterNewton() leaves λ free there instead, the trace's choice before this field. CorrectorReach reads any corrector's Newton callbacks.
  • palc_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.PALC, which override the trace's own (tangent, θ, dotθ, bls, step_control, orientation_check, corrector, orientation_dot). Do not override step_control, orientation_check, orientation_dot, θ, dotθ or bls: the trace's step shrinking, tangent orientation and geometry rest on them, and BifurcationKitSettings fields set them consistently.
  • continuation_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.ContinuationPar, which override the trace's own. Do not override newton_options, p_min, p_max, ds or the dsmin/dsmax pair: CorrectorReach converges a corrector on its own test and needs tol = 0, and the trace ends at p_max.

GameTracing.CorrectorSettings — Type
CorrectorSettings{E}(; reach, max_kl, logit_tolerance, settle_ulps, pin_tolerance, contraction_limit,
                     contraction_offset, noise_ratio, noise_limit, newton_iterations)

The corrector's controls (CorrectorReach); nothing switches a limit off. reach: a corrector that moves a logit further from the predictor than this times the predictor's own largest logit move is refused. shift_share: the share of that reach a redraw's predicted root shift may use (RootShift). max_kl: a corrected point whose largest per-logit probability KL from the predictor passes this is refused. A step's turn is limited by StepControl's turn_limit.

Convergence is scale-free, as in Gambit's tracer (path.cc:372, c_tol in log probabilities): a Newton correction that moves no logit more than logit_tolerance, at a precision within the relative pin_tolerance of its pin. Each logit also gets settle_ulps ulps of its own size (logit_corrections). A Float32 logit near 1e3 rounds by 1.2e-4, past the default tolerance, so without this allowance a Float32 trace stalls near λ 300 on 20x20 games. A correction that moves the logits more than contraction_limit times the one before it (plus logit_tolerance times contraction_offset) fails to contract; BifurcationKit's step growth reads the same rate (corrector_quality). Suspected noise is confirmed when the halved step's stalled correction is at least noise_ratio of the suspect's (corrected!). Halving keeps noise's corrections alike (ratio 1); it halves those of a predictor off the branch's tangent (ratio 1/2) and quarters those of its curvature (1/4). The default, 1/√2, splits 1/2 from 1. A stall counts as noise only within noise_limit times the noise floor (noise_floor): the larger of logit_tolerance and the largest settled correction so far. A corrector past it is far from any root, so the step halves at once. newton_iterations: Newton iterations per corrector (BifurcationKit's default, 25; Gambit's is 100).

GameTracing.LogitDistance — Type
LogitDistance{E}(; tolerance, settle_ulps, target_correction)

Measure a move by the largest change of any logit (logit_change). The corrector settles once a correction moves no logit more than tolerance past settle_ulps ulps of its own size (LogitSettled). Step control's target first correction is target_correction, or nothing to drop the term. GameTracing.LogitGame — Type
LogitGame(pass!, target_precision)

A logit game whose equilibria solve z = λ G(z, λ) at precision λ = 1 / temperature, G the payoff gains the logits scale. pass!(gain, gain_slope, gain_precision_slope, z, λ) writes G(z, λ), its Jacobian ∂G/∂z and its slope ∂G/∂λ into the three arrays, from one evaluation, and returns nothing. The arrays are views into the trace's own residual and Jacobian (BranchWorkspace), so a pass allocates nothing of its own. A pass that needs work arrays of its own gets them from pass_workspace. G must be finite at λ = 0, where the flat logits are the only equilibrium. target_precision is the precision a trace ends at.

GameTracing.NormalFormGains — Type
NormalFormGains(payoffs)

The pass! of a normal-form game with any number of strategies per player. payoffs[p][s] is player p's payoff at the pure profile s; every array has one type and one size, each player's strategy count along its own axis. Player p's logits are z_pk = log(π_pk / π_p1) for its strategies k ≥ 2, against its first strategy, stacked player by player (logit_ranges). Its gains are G_pk = U_p(k, π) - U_p(1, π), its expected payoffs against the rivals' mixed strategies, which do not depend on the precision. A pass writes into work arrays from pass_workspace; called without them, it builds its own.

GameTracing.PathFollowingSettings — Type
PathFollowingSettings{E}(; max_step, max_steps, distance, newton_iterations, contraction_limit, error_estimate,
                         target_contraction, target_turn, max_step_growth, orientation_check, max_refusals,
                         min_step_ulps, step_metric, linear_solver)

The controls of a trace on PathFollowing's pseudo-arclength continuation, an EngineSettings for logit_solve. The trace runs in [w; λ], the payoff gaps w = z / λ and the precision λ.

  • step_metric: a TraceGeometry, the norm of steps and turns in [w; λ] (GeometryWeights), reweighted at each accepted point.
  • max_step: the largest step in that norm; Inf sets none. The first step comes from the game (opening_step).
  • max_steps: accepted steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence.
  • distance: a CorrectorDistance, how the corrector measures a move: StrategyDistance (Fisher–Rao, in mixed strategies) or LogitDistance. It carries the corrector's settle tolerance and step control's target correction.
  • newton_iterations, contraction_limit, error_estimate: PathFollowing's Newton, its corrections under distance. ContractionEstimate(; safety) settles on the error left in the iterate, at most safety times the settle tolerance, often one iteration sooner than LastCorrection(). Its numbers convert to E.
  • target_contraction, target_turn, max_step_growth: PathFollowing's StepControl; nothing drops a target. The turn target is in radians under step_metric.
  • orientation_check: PathFollowing's orientation check. DeterminantSign refuses every step where sign det([J; tᵀ]) changes: the step ran back along the branch or crossed a bifurcation point. On a game with an exact pitchfork the trace then takes a side arm, as Gambit does. nothing switches the check off.
  • max_refusals: refused attempts in one step before the trace fails as TraceStuck.
  • min_step_ulps: a predictor that moves no coordinate more than this many ulps fails the trace as TraceStuck.
  • linear_solver: the LinearSolve.jl algorithm for the bordered systems.

GameTracing.StepControl — Type
StepControl{E}(; distance_limit, max_step_growth, turn_limit)

BifurcationKit's CorrectorQuality thresholds, which shrink a step whose corrector looks poor. distance_limit is the Euclidean length of a Newton step's logit part (Gambit's 0.4 in log probabilities); max_step_growth bounds a step's growth (Gambit's max_accel 1.1), and the step then shrinks as the distance or contraction nears its limit. turn_limit refuses a step whose tangent turns this many radians from the last one, Allgower and Georg's test (6.1.10) for a step across a fold onto another branch; 1 is under Gambit's quarter turn (t·newT < 0, path.cc). Without BifurcationKitSettings' orientation_check each tangent keeps the last one's direction, so a turn α reads min(α, π - α): the test catches turns near a right angle and misses a reversal. The corrector's contraction rate (CorrectorSettings) also slows growth. nothing switches a threshold off.

GameTracing.StepDistance — Type

The trace's step length in a state x = [w; λ] of k payoff gaps w at target precision P, at state_weight (the gaps' weight). Gaps and precision enter in the units P w and λ / P, so each distance weighs Δwᵢ² by P² and Δλ² by 1 / P²:

  • AveragedDistance: state_weight P² ‖Δw‖² / k + (1 - state_weight) (Δλ / P)².
  • SummedDistance: state_weight P² ‖Δw‖² + (Δλ / P)².
  • FisherDistance: state_weight P² Σ σ(zᵢ)σ(-zᵢ) Δwᵢ² + (Δλ / P)², with the Bernoulli Fisher weights of the logits z = λ w.
  • FisherLogPrecisionDistance: state_weight Σ σ(zᵢ)σ(-zᵢ) Δwᵢ² + (Δλ / (1 + λ))²: Fisher's payoff gains w beside the step in log(1 + λ) to first order, LogitNash's path variable. Neither term depends on P, so the target only caps the trace.

Both Fisher distances freeze their weights at each accepted point. The corrector's reach check measures in logits under every distance (within_reach).

GameTracing.trace_endpoint — Method
trace_endpoint(game, width, settings; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw, context)

Follow game's branch of width logits from the flat start (zero logits, the only root at precision zero) to game.target_precision, and return Ok(Traced) logits there or Err(TraceFailure) for the failure that stopped short (a TraceResult).

keep_going(logits, λ) runs at each accepted point before the next step's corrector, and false stops the trace. accept_corrected(logits, λ, converged) runs as each corrector ends, and false refuses a converged point, which halves the step. A trace that stops short fails with NoConvergence when it spends settings.max_steps and TraceStuck otherwise (a step below the float type's resolution, an unchanged accepted point, or a refusal); a non-finite start or a broken linear solve fails NotFinite or Singular. context rides on every failure.

redraw(logits, λ, shift) runs at each accepted point short of the target precision that keep_going passes, and returns nothing, a new game of the same type and target precision, or the ShiftedGame that shift(game) (RootShift) returned for one. That check tells whether a candidate game's root lies within the corrector's reach, so a caller can refine a draw that does not before it returns; the restart reuses its factorization of the new game's Jacobian. A new game replaces the map for the steps after that point: the trace restarts there along the new map's tangent, oriented by the old one, with the step it held, and its next corrector lands on the new map under the same step control. The endpoint, with no step after it, is never redrawn.

GameTracing.CorrectorReach — Type
CorrectorReach(accept_corrected, settings, geometry, target_precision, previous, predicted, step_length)

The trace's BifurcationKit callback_newton, which also decides the corrector's convergence: a settled iterate ends Newton's method converged (NewtonConverged), and a correction that fails to contract stops it (corrected!). Two such corrections in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move in logits and within noise_limit of the noise floor (noise_floor), are suspected rounding noise (noise), confirmed at the next halved step; the check then refuses every corrector (noise_confirmed). A non-finite residual stops the corrector, and so does any iterate whose logits are further from the predictor's than settings.reach times the predictor's own largest logit move (within_reach); the step then halves. A predictor within RESOLUTION_ULPS ulps of each of the point's coordinates (step_unresolved) refuses that corrector and every later one (unresolved): no smaller step can add a point, so the trace ends as TraceStuck, or as PrecisionExhausted when the corrector before it stopped past its reach (unresolved_noise). On a smooth branch that distance is the branch's curvature times ds² / 2, so halving brings any step under it, while an iterate that lands on another branch is far from it. A converged closing point is refused the same way past reach, past a largest probability KL KL(corrected ‖ predicted) of settings.max_kl (largest_kl). A predictor that did not move has no reach to compare. accept_corrected(logits, λ, converged) then sees every other closing point. previous and predicted hold the current step's start and predictor, iterate the current solve's last iterate, all updated in place; state (CorrectorState) holds the scalars that change between calls. geometry measures the step length at target_precision.

GameTracing.InPlaceMatrixBLS — Type
InPlaceMatrixBLS(factorization, rhs, solver)

BifurcationKit's MatrixBLS with the bordered matrix written into factorization's factors and LU-factored there (lu!), and the right-hand side solved in place in rhs; its solutions are bit-identical. Any real element type factors: LAPACK's for the BLAS floats, the generic LU otherwise. A triangular bordered matrix goes to \. solver is never read; it keeps PALC's update from replacing this solver with one built from the Newton options. The system is [J dR; ξu dzu' ξp dzp] [dX; dl] = [R; n], J the trace's width × width Jacobian.

The returned dX is a view of rhs, valid until the next solve. PALC's Newton step and tangent use it before then.

The RedrawSlot after a redraw hook's result redrawn, slot itself unless it swaps the game: nothing and the current game do not; a new game swaps as the ShiftedGame that check (RootShift) builds for it, and a ShiftedGame the hook returned must come from check.
