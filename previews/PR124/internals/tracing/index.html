guarded_trace(start, old_tangent, settings, finalise, accept_corrected, opening)

(; progress, step_length, accepted_step, exhausted, logit_accuracy, unresolved, exception) of a PALC segment on finalise.slot's game from start = [w; λ], the point of opening (the TraceProgress before it), for the steps settings.max_steps leaves: the progress after it (branch_progress), the step length its last corrector tried, the step of the last accepted point (the slot's, carried from an earlier segment when this one accepted none), the corrector's confirmed stall at rounding noise (exhausted_record, or nothing), the largest last correction of an accepted point, whether its predictor fell below the float type's resolution (step_unresolved), and nothing for a segment that ran, else the third-party failure it ended on. GameTracing.opening_tangent — Method
opening_tangent(game, start)

The branch's OpeningTangent at the flat start = [w; 0]. At precision zero play is uniform whatever the gaps, so ∂F/∂w is the identity and the tangent is (-∂F/∂λ, 1), read from one evaluation of the game's Jacobian. GameTracing.CorrectorReach — Type
CorrectorReach(accept_corrected, settings, geometry, target_precision, start, predicted, step_length, ranges)

The trace's BifurcationKit callback_newton, which also decides the corrector's convergence: a settled iterate ends Newton's method converged (NewtonConverged), and a correction that fails to contract stops it (corrected!). Two such corrections in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move in logits and within noise_limit of the noise floor (noise_floor), are suspected rounding noise (noise), confirmed at the next halved step; the check then refuses every corrector (noise_confirmed). A non-finite residual stops the corrector, and so does any iterate whose logits are further from the predictor's than settings.reach times the predictor's own largest logit move (within_reach); the step then halves. A predictor within RESOLUTION_ULPS ulps of each of the point's coordinates (step_unresolved) refuses that corrector and every later one (unresolved): no smaller step can add a point, so the trace ends as TraceStuck, or as PrecisionExhausted when the corrector before it stopped past its reach (unresolved_noise). On a smooth branch that distance is the branch's curvature times ds² / 2, so halving brings any step under it, while an iterate that lands on another branch is far from it. A converged closing point is refused the same way past reach, and past a player's KL KL(corrected ‖ predicted) of settings.max_kl over the players of ranges (largest_kl, kl_ranges). A predictor that did not move has no reach to compare. accept_corrected(logits, λ, converged) then sees every other closing point. predicted holds the current step's predictor, iterate the current solve's last iterate and stalled the iterate of the last stall or reach failure on record (noise, reach_failure, record_stall!), all updated in place, mutable copies of predicted and start in similar(predicted); state (CorrectorState) holds the scalars that change between calls. geometry measures the step length at target_precision.

GameTracing.TraceFinalise — Type
TraceFinalise(keep_going, redraw, slot, settings)

BifurcationKit's finalise_solution of one trace segment. An accepted point bitwise equal to the branch's last one (a step below the point's rounding) stops the trace, a stall, as does a point that closes a loop of the branch (looped). The trace goes on while keep_going(logits, precision) holds. Then redraw(logits, precision, shift) may return a new game, where shift is the point's RootShift check under settings: slot, a Ref to the current RedrawSlot (the constructor wraps the one it is given), takes it and the segment stops, for trace_endpoint to correct the point onto it and restart. A redraw that returns nothing or the current game swaps nothing. JULIA_DEBUG=trace lists every point.

GameTracing.corrected! — Method
corrected!(check, iterate, proceed)

check's verdict on a Newton iterate that its other tests let proceed, in logits z = λ w. The first iterate is the solve's start and never settles. Each later one is a correction Δz from the iterate before: it settles (NewtonConverged) when settled by the part of Δz past each logit's settle_ulps. A correction past contraction_limit times the one before (plus logit_tolerance · contraction_offset) fails to contract, Gambit's test (path.cc), and stops the corrector, so the step halves. Two in a row, each larger than the predictor's own move and within noise_limit of the noise floor (noise_floor), are suspected noise (noise), the iterate in check.stalled; the step still halves. A second such solve before any settles, its correction at least noise_ratio of the suspect's, confirms it (noise_confirmed): halving shrinks the corrections of a predictor past a fold, not noise. Every later solve refuses.

GameTracing.looped — Method
looped(history, current, target_precision)

Whether current, a point with gaps and param that follows the branch records history, closes a loop of the branch (an isola). Gates: the precision tangent changes sign between the anchor and current (a closed curve returns to its starting precision), and the tangents of history have turned through at least 2π in total (tangent_turn), as a closed curve must (Fenchel's theorem). Then Brent's cycle detection: the anchor is the record at the largest power-of-two index not after the last record (indices from 1), and the trace has looped when current lies closer to the anchor than the last step is long, with the anchor at least two records before current. Distances are plain Euclidean on [P gaps; param / P], P the target_precision, as TraceFinalise holds no TraceGeometry.

GameTracing.step_unresolved — Method

Whether the predictor predicted moves no coordinate of start by more than RESOLUTION_ULPS · eps(E) of its own size: the ODE solvers' per-coordinate error test, |Δz_i| ≤ abstol + reltol |z_i|, with abstol zero. Coordinates of different scales (payoff gaps near 1, precision up to 1e6) each count, and any move of a zero coordinate resolves.

GameTracing.RootShift — Type
RootShift(point, border, limit)
RootShift(point, step, border, settings)

The check a redraw hook receives at the accepted point point = [w; λ]. Called on a candidate game, it returns the ShiftedGame: one evaluation of the game's map and Jacobian at point, the bordered system under the corrector's border factored once, and the root shift δ from [∂F/∂x; borderᵀ] δ = [-F(point); 0], one Newton step of the bordered corrector. The old map's residual at an accepted point is within the corrector's tolerance, so -F(point) stands for F_old(point) - F_new(point). The shift is within when it moves no logit further than limit: settings.shift_share of the corrector's reach on the next step, the predictor step (CorrectorSettings), or nothing when the reach is off, and every game passes. A hook returns the ShiftedGame to install its game, and the restart reuses its factorization.

source
GameTracing.ShiftedGame — Type
ShiftedGame(game, point, factorization, shift, shift_move, within)

A candidate game at the accepted point point = [w; λ] (RootShift): its bordered Jacobian [∂F/∂x; bᵀ] there, factored, its root shift δ, that shift's largest logit move shift_move, and whether the move is within the check's limit. A non-finite map or Jacobian at point has no factorization (nothing), a NaN shift and is never within; a restart on it fails as LAPACK's check of a non-finite matrix does, at the swap point.

GameTracing.swapped — Method
swapped(slot, check, redrawn)

The RedrawSlot after a redraw hook's result redrawn, slot itself unless it swaps the game: nothing and the current game do not; a new game swaps as the ShiftedGame that check (RootShift) builds for it, and a ShiftedGame the hook returned must come from check. A swap sets pending, the factorization and the root shift the restart reads; a shift beyond the corrector's reach (not within) is dropped, so a root out of reach fails stuck at the swap instead of jumping branches. A game of another type, or with another target precision, is a caller error: the trace's precision would change meaning.

source
GameTracing.projection_step — Method
projection_step(game, logits, precision, geometry)

One minimum-norm Gauss–Newton step from x = [logits / λ; λ] onto game's branch F(x) = 0 in payoff gaps, in geometry's plain norm W at the game's target precision (plain_weights): dx = -W⁻¹ Jᵀ (J W⁻¹ Jᵀ)⁻¹ F. It is the minimum-norm solution of (J W^-½) y = -F by pivoted QR, scaled back by W^-½; the normal matrix is never formed. The step moves λ as well, so it stays defined at a fold, where a correction at fixed λ is singular. Payoff gaps w = z / λ exist at every finite λ ≠ 0, so λ must be one.
