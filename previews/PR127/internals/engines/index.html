Internals: engines

The PathFollowing engine and pygambit's logit_solve interface.

GameTracing.FisherRaoChange — Type
FisherRaoChange(ranges)

The largest Fisher–Rao distance any player's mixed strategy moves between two points [w; λ], each player's logits z = λ w in its range of ranges, against its first strategy (fisher_rao_spread).

source
GameTracing.FisherRaoSettled — Type
FisherRaoSettled(ranges, tolerance, rounding)

PathFollowing's converged under StrategyDistance, each player's logits in its range of ranges. The last correction, times Newton's scale, moved no player's strategy further than tolerance plus rounding times its logits' spread (fisher_rao_spread). That product bounds the Fisher–Rao distance a relative rounding of rounding in each logit moves the strategy.

source
GameTracing.GeometryWeights — Type
GeometryWeights(geometry)

PathFollowing's PointWeights for geometry over points [w; λ], at target precision one (StepDistance). Each gap weighs coordinate_weight, or for a Fisher distance state_weight σ(z)σ(-z) at its logit z = λ w. The precision weighs precision_weight, or 1 / (1 + λ)² for FisherLogPrecisionDistance.

LogitSettled(tolerance, rounding)

PathFollowing's converged over points [w; λ]: the last correction, times Newton's scale, moved no logit more than tolerance past rounding times that logit's own size (logit_corrections).

GameTracing.fisher_rao_spread — Method

A player's move from from to to, its logits in range, and its logits' spread at to. The move is the Fisher–Rao distance 4 asin(h / 2), h the Euclidean distance between the square roots of the two strategies: a geodesic on the probability simplex, at most π. A small move of logits Δz has length √Var_π(Δz). The spread is the root mean square √(Σ π z²) of the logits z = λ w[range] at to under their own mixed strategy π.

GameTracing.log_normalizer — Method

The log normalizer log(1 + Σ exp(z)) of a player's logits z = λ w[range] at a point [w; λ], in two parts: the largest logit or zero, and the log of the sum shifted by it. Their sum rounds away the gaps between large logits.

GameTracing.opening_step — Method

The first step of a trace of gains: 1 / payoff_range(gains) in λ, where no logit z = λ w has moved by more than one. Step control then grows it by up to max_step_growth per step. Equal or non-finite payoffs step one; a non-finite start fails anyway (NotFiniteStart).

GameTracing.gambit_settings — Method
gambit_settings(first_step, max_accel)

BifurcationKitSettings for pygambit's step arguments: first_step is the first arclength step and max_accel caps each step's growth (aggressiveness = max_accel - 1). The trace steps in FisherLogPrecisionDistance, whose precision term is the step in log(1 + λ), so a trace to a far precision opens at the scale of a near one.
