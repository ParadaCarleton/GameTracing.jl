GameTracing.GameTracing — Module
GameTracing

Homotopy continuation for logit quantal response equilibria. trace_endpoint follows the branch of a logit game from zero precision (infinite temperature), where the only equilibrium is the flat one, up to a target precision with BifurcationKit's pseudo-arclength continuation (PALC), across folds. logit_solve and its kin run on BifurcationKit (BifurcationKitSettings) or on PathFollowing (PathFollowingSettings). Failures are values (TraceFailure), never exceptions.

GameTracing.BifurcationKitSettings — Type
BifurcationKitSettings{E}(; max_step, aggressiveness, step_metric,
                 corrector, max_steps, tangent, bordered_solver, step_control, orientation_check, palc_corrector,
                 palc_options, continuation_options)

BifurcationKit PALC controls for trace_endpoint, in (w, λ) with w = logits / λ the payoff gaps and λ the precision. Step sizes are arclength in step_metric's norm (StepDistance). The defaults are gambit_settings(E, GAMBIT_MAX_ACCEL): Gambit's growth limit. The first step comes from the game (opening_step).

  • max_step: the largest arclength step, which also caps the first. A step whose corrector fails halves; the trace ends as TraceStuck once its predictor moves the point no more than a few ulps of its size (step_unresolved). BifurcationKit's dsmin is zero: its halving stops at a zero step. After the predictor stops moving, BifurcationKit still halves to zero through the subnormals, about 150 halvings of one refused residual evaluation each.
  • aggressiveness (BifurcationKit's a): without a step_control growth limit, a step grows by 1 + a ((N - n) / N)² after a corrector of n of its N = corrector.newton_iterations iterations. The default is GAMBIT_MAX_ACCEL - 1, the growth limit's largest growth.
  • corrector: a CorrectorSettings.
  • step_metric: a TraceGeometry, the norm a trace steps in and its state weight. The default is default_step_metric, both engines' default. The first tangent is the exact tangent at the flat start (opening_tangent).
  • max_steps: continuation steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence.
  • tangent: BifurcationKit's tangent algorithm, passed to PALC as is. Bordered() (the default) solves the bordered Jacobian system. Secant() extrapolates the last two points and saves that solve. Do not use it where a small payoff perturbation unfolds a pitchfork: there it crosses onto another arm (the test "a pitchfork unfolded by a payoff perturbation"). Over 40 random unfoldings at payoff scale 1e-4 it kept the branch in none, Bordered() in all 40.
  • bordered_solver: a BorderedSolver.
  • step_control: a StepControl, or nothing for none.
  • orientation_check orients a bordered tangent by the determinant of the bordered matrix. Only MatrixSolve has a determinant, so BorderingSolve needs orientation_check = false; the constructor throws otherwise.
  • palc_corrector: PALC's corrector keyword, nothing by default, which is BifurcationKit's own Newton method (newton_palc; it stops on leaving [0, P], P the target precision). BifurcationKit.FreeParameterNewton() leaves λ free there instead, the trace's choice before this field. CorrectorReach reads any corrector's Newton callbacks.
  • palc_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.PALC. A keyword the trace sets itself (tangent, θ, dotθ, bls, step_control, orientation_check, corrector, orientation_dot) throws an ArgumentError at the trace: the step shrinking, tangent orientation and geometry rest on them. Set them through the fields above.
  • continuation_options: a NamedTuple of further keywords for BifurcationKit.ContinuationPar. A keyword the trace sets itself (continuation_options lists them) throws the same way: CorrectorReach needs tol = 0, and the trace ends at p_max.

GameTracing.CorrectorSettings — Type
CorrectorSettings{E}(; reach, max_kl, logit_tolerance, settle_ulps, pin_tolerance, contraction_limit,
                     contraction_offset, noise_ratio, noise_limit, newton_iterations)

The corrector's controls (CorrectorReach); nothing switches a limit off. reach: a corrector that moves a logit further from the predictor than this times the predictor's own largest logit move is refused. shift_share: the share of that reach a redraw's predicted root shift may use (RootShift). max_kl: a corrected point that moves any player's mixed strategy further from the predictor's than this KL is refused (largest_kl); the game's pass must define logit_ranges. A step's turn is limited by StepControl's turn_limit.

Convergence is scale-free, as in Gambit's tracer (path.cc:372, c_tol in log probabilities): a Newton correction that moves no logit more than logit_tolerance, at a precision within the relative pin_tolerance of its pin. Each logit also gets settle_ulps ulps of its own size (logit_corrections). A Float32 logit near 1e3 rounds by 1.2e-4, past the default tolerance, so without this allowance a Float32 trace stalls near λ 300 on 20x20 games. A correction that moves the logits more than contraction_limit times the one before it (plus logit_tolerance times contraction_offset) fails to contract; BifurcationKit's step growth reads the same rate (corrector_quality). Suspected noise is confirmed when the halved step's stalled correction is at least noise_ratio of the suspect's (corrected!). Halving keeps noise's corrections alike (ratio 1); it halves those of a predictor off the branch's tangent (ratio 1/2) and quarters those of its curvature (1/4). The default, 1/√2, splits 1/2 from 1. A stall counts as noise only within noise_limit times the noise floor (noise_floor): the larger of logit_tolerance and the largest settled correction so far. A corrector past it is far from any root, so the step halves at once. newton_iterations: Newton iterations per corrector (BifurcationKit's default, 25; Gambit's is 100).

GameTracing.LogitDistance — Type
LogitDistance{E}(; tolerance, settle_ulps, target_correction)

Measure a move by the largest change of any logit (logit_change). The corrector settles once a correction moves no logit more than tolerance past settle_ulps ulps of its own size (LogitSettled). Step control's target first correction is target_correction, or nothing to drop the term. Do not use it to high precision when a strategy leaves the support: its logit grows like λ, and its corrections, λ times the square of the others', hold the steps short. A set target must lie above tolerance (settle_tolerance).

GameTracing.LogitGame — Type
LogitGame(pass!, target_precision)

A logit game whose equilibria solve z = λ G(z, λ) at precision λ = 1 / temperature, G the payoff gains the logits scale. pass!(gain, gain_slope, gain_precision_slope, z, λ) writes G(z, λ), its Jacobian ∂G/∂z and its slope ∂G/∂λ into the three arrays, from one evaluation, and returns nothing. The arrays are views into the trace's own residual and Jacobian (BranchWorkspace), so a pass allocates nothing of its own. A pass that needs work arrays of its own gets them from pass_workspace. G must be finite at λ = 0, where the flat logits are the only equilibrium. target_precision is the precision a trace ends at, non-negative and finite (ArgumentError otherwise).

LogitQREProfile(gains, lam, logits)

One point of a normal-form game's logit QRE branch, as pygambit's LogitQREMixedStrategyProfile: the precision lam (Gambit's λ, π ∝ exp(λ u), the trace's precision) and profile, each player's mixed strategy (mixed_strategies). logits are the trace's log odds against each player's first strategy; they stay finite where a probability underflows.

GameTracing.NormalFormGains — Type
NormalFormGains(payoffs)

The pass! of a normal-form game with any number of strategies per player. payoffs[p][s] is player p's payoff at the pure profile s; every array has one type and one size, each player's strategy count along its own axis. Player p's logits are z_pk = log(π_pk / π_p1) for its strategies k ≥ 2, against its first strategy, stacked player by player (logit_ranges). Its gains are G_pk = U_p(k, π) - U_p(1, π), its expected payoffs against the rivals' mixed strategies, which do not depend on the precision. A pass writes into work arrays from pass_workspace; called without them, it builds its own.

GameTracing.PathFollowingSettings — Type
PathFollowingSettings{E}(; max_step, max_steps, distance, newton_iterations, contraction_limit, error_estimate,
                         target_contraction, target_turn, max_step_growth, reach, orientation_check, split_check,
                         max_refusals, min_step_ulps, step_metric, linear_solver)

The controls of a trace on PathFollowing's pseudo-arclength continuation, an EngineSettings for logit_solve. The trace runs in [w; λ], the payoff gaps w = z / λ and the precision λ.

  • step_metric: a TraceGeometry, the norm of steps and turns in [w; λ] (trace_metric) at target precision one, frozen at each accepted point. The default, default_step_metric, is gambit_settings' too.
  • max_step: the largest step in that norm; Inf sets none. The first step comes from the game (opening_step).
  • max_steps: accepted steps before the trace fails as NoConvergence.
  • distance: a CorrectorDistance, how the corrector measures a move: StrategyDistance (Fisher–Rao, in mixed strategies) or LogitDistance. It carries the corrector's settle tolerance and step control's target correction.
  • newton_iterations, contraction_limit, error_estimate: PathFollowing's Newton, its corrections under distance. ContractionEstimate(; safety) settles on the error left in the iterate, at most safety times the settle tolerance, often one iteration sooner than LastCorrection(). Its numbers convert to E.
  • target_contraction, target_turn, max_step_growth, reach: PathFollowing's StepControl; nothing drops a target. The turn target is in radians under step_metric. A converged point farther than reach step lengths from its predictor refuses the step, as one that jumped to another branch, unless distance puts the move within the corrector's settle tolerance. Inf switches the refusal off.
  • orientation_check: PathFollowing's orientation check. DeterminantSign refuses every step where sign det([J; tᵀ]) changes: the step ran back along the branch or crossed a bifurcation point. nothing switches the check off.
  • split_check: PathFollowing's HalfStepCheck{E}, or nothing to switch it off. A step with factor at least trigger runs again in two half steps, which must reach its point within tolerance in distance. The default trigger is 1.3 (default_split_check), and the tolerance 10 times distance's settle tolerance. A zero settle tolerance has no default: pass split_check. Without the check, at the default step control, 4 of 20,000 random two-action games and 3 of 1200 unfolded pitchforks left the principal branch on a step that passed every other test. Tolerance evidence, at growth 2 and the summed metric, with StrategyDistance in Float32 and Float64. A sweep with the reach on and off: 203 seeds of 20 two-action games, and battle of the sexes plus 1e-4 to 1e-1 randn payoffs, 300 draws per scale. The half steps flagged all 22 jumps and 148 of 196,594 steps that kept the branch (0.075%). Kept steps reached their point within 2.5e-5 at the 99.99th percentile. Each jump failed or landed at least 0.27 away. So the 5e-4 tolerance lies 20 times above that percentile and 550 times under the nearest jump. At the default step control, trigger 1.3 refused 21 of 565,078 kept steps in the hunt (0.004%).
  • max_refusals: refused attempts in one step before the trace fails as TraceStuck.
  • min_step_ulps: a predictor that moves no coordinate more than this many ulps fails the trace as TraceStuck.
  • linear_solver: the LinearSolve.jl algorithm for the bordered systems.

GameTracing.PrecisionTerm — Type

The precision's term in FisherDistance: TargetPrecisionTerm (Δλ / P)², or LogPrecisionTerm (Δλ / (1 + λ))², the step in log(1 + λ) to first order, LogitNash's path variable. The log term does not depend on P, so the target only caps the trace. The plain distances weigh the precision at the target alone.

GameTracing.StepControl — Type
StepControl{E}(; distance_limit, max_step_growth, turn_limit)

BifurcationKit's CorrectorQuality thresholds, which shrink a step whose corrector looks poor. distance_limit is the Euclidean length of a Newton step's logit part (Gambit's 0.4 in log probabilities); max_step_growth bounds a step's growth (Gambit's max_accel 1.1), and the step then shrinks as the distance or contraction nears its limit. turn_limit refuses a step whose tangent turns this many radians from the last one, Allgower and Georg's test (6.1.10) for a step across a fold onto another branch; 1 is under Gambit's quarter turn (t·newT < 0, path.cc). Without BifurcationKitSettings' orientation_check each tangent keeps the last one's direction, so a turn α reads min(α, π - α): the test catches turns near a right angle and misses a reversal. The corrector's contraction rate (CorrectorSettings) also slows growth. nothing switches a threshold off.

source
GameTracing.StepDistance — Type

The trace's step length in a state x = [w; λ] of k payoff gaps w at target precision P, at state_weight (the gaps' weight). Gaps and precision enter in the units P w and λ / P, so each plain distance weighs Δwᵢ² by P² and Δλ² by 1 / P²:

  • AveragedDistance: state_weight P² ‖Δw‖² / k + (1 - state_weight) (Δλ / P)².
  • SummedDistance: state_weight P² ‖Δw‖² + (Δλ / P)².
  • FisherDistance: state_weight Σₚ Δzₚᵀ Gₚ Δzₚ plus a PrecisionTerm: the Fisher information of the strategy profile at the logits z = λ w, pulled back to the state through Δz = λ Δw + w Δλ. Player p's block Gₚ = diag(qₚ) - qₚqₚᵀ spans its logits (logit_ranges); FisherMetric applies it.

The Fisher distance freezes G, w and λ at each accepted point. The trace's plain products (trace_dot) measure it as SummedDistance. The corrector's reach check measures in logits under every distance (within_reach).

GameTracing.StrategyDistance — Type
StrategyDistance{E}(; tolerance, settle_ulps, target_correction)

Measure a move by the largest Fisher–Rao distance any player's mixed strategy travels (FisherRaoChange). A strategy counts by its probability, so one that has left the support stops counting. The corrector settles once a correction moves no strategy further than tolerance past the distance settle_ulps ulps of each logit move it (FisherRaoSettled). Step control's target first correction is target_correction, or nothing to drop the term. A set target must lie above tolerance (settle_tolerance).

GameTracing.TraceFailure — Type
TraceFailure(cause, context)

A trace that did not reach its target: cause names why and context carries the last accepted logits and precision. A trace_endpoint trace that left its start also carries that point's payoff gaps w, the tangent there in logits (logit_tangent, dz = λ dw + w dλ) and in payoff gaps (gap_tangent, tangent_precision), and the step it held; a non-finite start has none of these. A PrecisionExhausted failure also carries the Newton iterate stalled_iterate = [w; λ] whose correction in logits stalled, at precision lambda, and the stalled_pin and stalled_arclength of its PALC step. The caller's own context fields sit beside these (failure_context).

GameTracing.TraceGeometry — Type
TraceGeometry{E}(; distance, state_weight, precision_term)

The norm a trace steps in: its StepDistance, state_weight, the gaps' weight against the precision's, and the Fisher distance's PrecisionTerm. A plain distance takes TargetPrecisionTerm only; another throws an ArgumentError. The default is both engines' (default_step_metric): the Fisher distance at state_weight = 0.1 with LogPrecisionTerm. A plain distance defaults to TargetPrecisionTerm, its only term.

state_weight = 0.1 serves both precision terms (benchmark/results/tune-4dd74d7-summary.txt, weights 0.003 to 0.9). Each term reached every categorical game at 0.1. The target term took its fewest steps there (304); the log term took 408, against 396 at 0.3. Below 0.03 the log term exhausted at the folding 3x3 game and at UNFOLDED_PITCHFORK_GAME.

GameTracing.logit_ranges — Function
logit_ranges(pass!)

Each player's range in the stacked logits of pass!'s game: the logits of its strategies against its reference strategy, one UnitRange{Int} per player in a tuple or vector, tiling the logits in order (checked_ranges). GameTracing.logit_solve_branch — Method
logit_solve_branch(gains; maxregret, max_accel, maximum_precision, settings)

pygambit's logit_solve_branch: every accepted point of the principal branch of the normal-form game gains, from the first step to the first point where no player's regret passes maxregret times the payoff range. The trace runs to maximum_precision at most, and fails NoConvergence with reason :maximum_precision if it ends there unsettled; any other failure of the trace is returned as is. maxregret defaults to sqrt(eps(E)) in the trace's element type E. max_accel and settings are logit_solve_lambda's.

GameTracing.logit_solve_lambda — Method
logit_solve_lambda(gains, lam; max_accel, settings)

pygambit's logit_solve_lambda: the LogitQREProfile on the principal branch of the normal-form game gains (NormalFormGains) at each precision in lam, a number or a vector, in lam's order, or the first trace's failure. max_accel caps the trace's step growth (gambit_settings); settings, an EngineSettings, replaces it. pygambit's first_step has no counterpart: every trace's first step comes from the game (opening_step). PathFollowingSettings trace once, their steps stopping at each precision in turn. Every precision must be non-negative and finite in the trace's element type (ArgumentError otherwise). Profiles take float of the payoffs' element type (Float64 for integer payoffs), and settings must share it (ArgumentError otherwise).

GameTracing.trace_endpoint — Method
trace_endpoint(game, width, settings; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw, context)

Follow game's branch of width logits from the flat start (zero logits, the only root at precision zero) to game.target_precision, and return Ok(Traced) logits there or Err(TraceFailure) for the failure that stopped short (a TraceResult).

keep_going(logits, λ) runs at each accepted point before the next step's corrector, and false stops the trace. accept_corrected(logits, λ, converged) runs as each corrector ends, and false refuses a converged point, which halves the step. A trace that stops short fails with NoConvergence when it spends settings.max_steps and TraceStuck otherwise (a step below the float type's resolution, an unchanged accepted point, or a refusal); a non-finite start or a broken linear solve fails NotFinite or Singular. A zero target returns the flat start, with no step. context rides on every failure.

redraw(logits, λ, shift) runs at each accepted point short of the target precision that keep_going passes, and returns nothing, a new game of the same type and target precision, or the ShiftedGame that shift(game) (RootShift) returned for one. That check tells whether a candidate game's root lies within the corrector's reach, so a caller can refine a draw that does not before it returns; the restart reuses its factorization of the new game's Jacobian. A new game replaces the map for the steps after that point: the trace restarts there along the new map's tangent, oriented by the old one, with the step it held, and its next corrector lands on the new map under the same step control. The endpoint, with no step after it, is never redrawn.
