Internals: games and geometry

Logit games, normal-form payoffs, bordered solves, step metrics and the Fisher metric.

GameTracing.BranchWorkspace — Type
BranchWorkspace(game, point)

A residual, Jacobian, logits and game's pass work arrays (pass_workspace) for branch points like point = [w; λ], refilled by every call of the one callable that owns it. BifurcationKit consumes each result before calling that callable again, so the fused system and the Jacobian each own one workspace per trace; a shared one would let J overwrite a residual FJ returned.

GameTracing.GameResidual — Type
GameResidual(game)

BifurcationKit's F(x, pin) of game's branch in payoff gaps, with the precision carried as the state's last coordinate: x = [w; λ], F = [w - G(λ w, λ); λ - pin]. The logits are z = λ w, so F = 0 is z = λ G(z, λ), and F stays regular at λ = 0. pin is BifurcationKit's continuation parameter, which only pins λ. Each call fills a fresh BranchWorkspace: BifurcationKit keeps its problem's F apart from the fused system.

GameTracing.opening_step — Method

The first arclength step of a trace of game in E, at most max_step: 1 / gap_bound(game.pass!). Under LogPrecisionTerm a step h from λ = 0 moves λ by at most h, so no logit z = λ w moves by more than one. Step control grows or shrinks it from there. A bound without a positive finite inverse (zero, non-finite or subnormal) steps one; a non-finite start fails anyway (NotFinite).

GameTracing.contract_axes! — Method
contract_axes!(target, source, dims, group, weights)

Contract the adjacent axes group of the tensor source of size dims, read in linear order, into target: the slices along the group, each times its joint weight in weights (group_weights!), summed. Return the new size, one along group. A block past the group holds one stride-long run per slice. The inner loop runs along a run when it is at least as long as the slices are many, else across the blocks; each entry adds its slices in order either way.

GameTracing.InPlaceMatrixBLS — Type
InPlaceMatrixBLS(factorization, rhs, solver)

BifurcationKit's MatrixBLS with the bordered matrix written into factorization's factors and LU-factored there (lu!), and the right-hand side solved in place in rhs; its solutions are bit-identical. Any real element type factors: LAPACK's for the BLAS floats, the generic LU otherwise. A triangular bordered matrix goes to \. solver is never read; it keeps PALC's update from replacing this solver with one built from the Newton options. The system is [J dR; ξu dzu' ξp dzp] [dX; dl] = [R; n], J the trace's width × width Jacobian.

The returned dX is a view of rhs, valid until the next solve. PALC's Newton step and tangent use it before then.

source
BifurcationKit.bordered_tangent_and_sign — Method

The Bordered tangent of PALC and the sign of the bordered matrix's determinant that its orientation_check reads, from one LU: the matrix is built as MatrixBLS builds it, from PALC's θ and dotθ, factored in bls's buffers (lu!), the tangent solves with that factorization, and the sign is its determinant's (logabsdet). A zero pivot makes the matrix singular: the sign and the tangent are zero, which PALC's NaN checks refuse as a step.

GameTracing.FisherMetric — Type
FisherMetric(ranges, state_weight, precision_term, target_precision, palc_state_weight)

FisherDistance as an operator M on displacements [Δw; Δλ] at a point [w; λ], the logits z = λ w of each player in its range of ranges (logit_ranges), at PALC state weight θ = palc_state_weight:

ΔxᵀMΔx = (state_weight Σₚ Δzₚᵀ Gₚ Δzₚ + (ω(λ) - (1 - θ)) Δλ²) / θ, with Δz = λ Δw + w Δλ.

Gₚ = diag(qₚ) - qₚqₚᵀ is the Fisher information of player p's mixed strategy in its logits, qₚ the probabilities of its strategies past the reference: the Hessian of log(1 + Σ exp zₚ). A player with one logit has σ(z)σ(-z). PALC adds (1 - θ) Δλ² itself, so its norm gives Δλ² the weight ω(λ). c(λ) is precision_term's weight toward target_precision P (precision_term_root), and ω(λ) is c(λ) while c(λ) ≥ c(P), up to P for the log term. Past P the log term's c(λ) - (1 - θ) can turn negative, so ω(λ) is c(λ) + (1 - θ) (1 - c(λ) / c(P)) (precision_dot_root). The trace ends at P, so only its last point is past it. PathFollowing passes θ = 1, which gives ω = c. 1 - θ must lie under c(P), or the constructor throws an ArgumentError. PathFollowing.frozen_metric freezes M at a point (FrozenFisher).

source
GameTracing.FrozenFisher — Type
FrozenFisher(metric, point, probabilities, references, pivots, state_weight, palc_state_weight, root)

metric (FisherMetric) frozen at point = [w; λ]: each strategy's probability by its logit, each player's reference probability, and each player's pivot, its most probable strategy's logit index or 0 for its reference. The products center each player's logit moves on its pivot's move. A move common to every strategy but an extinct reference then keeps its Fisher length √p_ref, which a raw Σ qᵢΔzᵢ² - (Σ qᵢΔzᵢ)² rounds away. The buffers follow point's storage. state_weight and palc_state_weight are metric's, and root holds precision_dot_root at point as its only entry. The products read these values in E and metric's ranges, nothing else.

GameTracing.kl_share — Method

One strategy's share π_from (eᵈ - 1 - d) of a player's KL, d = log π_to - log π_from, from the log probabilities from and to. The shares sum to the KL as both distributions sum to one. Each share is second order in d, so the sum keeps its relative accuracy at a small KL. Past |d| = 1/2, where log1pmx loses nothing to cancellation, the share is π_to - π_from (1 + d), which stays finite where eᵈ overflows. Where π_from rounds to 0, the share is π_to, its limit as π_from → 0, also at an infinite d.

GameTracing.log_normalizer — Method

A player's pivot and log normalizer at a point [w; λ], its logits z = λ w[range] against its reference. The pivot is its most probable strategy: the index of its largest logit, or 0 for its reference. The pivot comes from the gaps w, largest for λ > 0 and smallest for λ < 0, so an overflowing product λ wᵢ cannot choose it. The normalizer is log Σ exp(zᵢ - z_pivot) over its strategies and its reference (centered_logit). Each logit enters as λ (wᵢ - w_pivot), which keeps its relative accuracy at a large common logit. No centered logit is positive, so the normalizer is finite. log1p of the other strategies' sum keeps its relative accuracy where that sum is under eps.

GameTracing.player_apply! — Method

Write player player's block of M v, gap_scale times (G J v)ᵢ = qᵢ dᵢ (d as in player_inner), into out[range], and return its Σᵢ wᵢ qᵢ dᵢ. The deviations average to zero, so Σᵢ qᵢ dᵢ is the reference's π_ref (pivot + mean). The pivot's entry closes that sum over the other rounded entries, and the gaps enter less the pivot's gap. A common move against an extinct reference then stays as small as its Fisher length, which rounded entries summed apart would bury.

The block's sum is within eps ((n + L) Σᵢ |outᵢ| + |gap_scale| π_ref Σᵢ qᵢ (|λ Δwᵢ| + |wᵢ Δλ|)) of M's, for n strategies and L the largest |log π| among them. The first term is the entries' rounding and the second the logit moves'. The bound holds under Higham's standard model fl(x ∘ y) = (x ∘ y) (1 + δ): while every probability, product and partial sum is a normal float or zero. Outside that domain, the function promises nothing. Entries of size s hold no nonzero sum under about eps s, so a large differential move can sum to zero.

GameTracing.precision_dot_root — Method

The root √(ω(λ) - (1 - θ)) of the weight of Δλ² in metric's dot at precision precision, before PALC's θ divides it (FisherMetric). While c(λ) ≥ c(P) the weight is c(λ) - (1 - θ). Past P PALC's share shrinks with the log term, to (1 - θ) c(λ) / c(P). Both are c(λ) (1 - (1 - θ) / max(c(λ), c(P))), whose root √c(λ) √(1 - …) is positive for every finite λ, as 1 - θ < c(P). The products apply the root to each side, (root Δλ) (root Δλ'). share_complement gives 1 - … within eps |1 - …| + 8 eps². While 1 - … is far above 8 eps², and each side's root Δλ and their product are normal floats, the dot's precision term keeps its relative accuracy.

source
GameTracing.precision_term_root — Method

The root √c(λ) of the weight of Δλ² that precision_term gives at precision precision toward target_precision: TargetPrecisionTerm 1 / P, LogPrecisionTerm 1 / (1 + |λ|). Over λ ∈ [0, P] both are smallest at λ = P. The root is positive for every finite λ; c itself underflows past 1 / √floatmin.

source
GameTracing.share_complement — Method

1 - (1 - θ) D² for θ = palc_state_weight and D = high + low (weight_denominator): one minus PALC's share of the weight of Δλ². Near a share of one, the subtraction cancels. Fast2Sum (two_hilo_diff, as θ ≤ 1) keeps the rounding error of 1 - θ, and FMA (two_prod) keeps the rounding error of each product. While 1 - θ ≤ 1 / D², every product is finite and the result is within eps |r| + 8 eps² of the exact value r.
