Internals: engines

The PathFollowing engine and pygambit's logit_solve interface.

GameTracing.FisherRaoChange — Type
FisherRaoChange(ranges)

The Fisher–Rao distance a profile moves between two points [w; λ] on the product of the players' simplices: the root sum of squares of each player's distance (fisher_rao_spread). Each player's logits z = λ w lie in its range of ranges (logit_ranges), against its first strategy. Tuple ranges make it isbits.

source
GameTracing.FisherRaoSettled — Type
FisherRaoSettled(ranges, tolerance, rounding)

PathFollowing's converged under StrategyDistance, each player's logits in its range of ranges (logit_ranges). The last correction, times Newton's scale, moved the profile (FisherRaoChange) no further than tolerance plus rounding times the root sum of squares of the players' logit spreads (fisher_rao_spread). That product bounds the distance a relative rounding of rounding in each logit moves the profile. Tuple ranges make it isbits.

source
GameTracing.LogitSettled — Type
LogitSettled(tolerance, rounding)

PathFollowing's converged over points [w; λ]: the last correction's logit move, times Newton's scale, is at most tolerance past rounding times the logits' own size (logit_corrections).

GameTracing.PositivePrecision — Type
PositivePrecision(accept_corrected)

PathFollowing's accept: it refuses a corrected point at precision zero or below, and one that accept_corrected refuses (corrected_accepted, converged true). The principal branch meets precision zero only at its start, so such a point went back along it; the refusal retries a shorter step. AcceptAll() refuses no other point, and builds no logits.

GameTracing.default_split_check — Method

PathFollowingSettings' default split check: trigger 1.3 and tolerance 10 times distance's settle tolerance. At growth 1.1 a step held by a step-control target rides at f near 1. So trigger 1 fired on up to 67% of logitnashspeed's steps and cost up to 33% per game (median). Trigger 1.3 fired on 0.4-2.7% of them and cost at most 2%. A hunt over 20,000 two-action games and 1200 unfolded pitchforks found the same 7 jumps in Float32 and Float64. Trigger 1.3 fired on and refused each; the smallest jump f was 1.41. At growth 2 with the summed metric, jumps came as low as f 1.1: a looser step control needs trigger 1. A zero settle tolerance gives the tolerance no scale, so it throws an ArgumentError that asks for an explicit `splitcheck`.

GameTracing.engine_endpoint — Method
engine_endpoint(game, start, pass_work, settings::PathFollowingSettings, callbacks, context)

trace_endpoint on PathFollowing from the finite start = [w; 0] (trace_start) on game's pass work pass_work, to a nonzero target: one trace whose steps stop there (stepped_landing!!), with callbacks = (; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw). accept_corrected sees converged points only (converged is true), and its false refuses the point, which shrinks the step (PositivePrecision). A stop short of the target fails TraceStuck for PathFollowing's Unresolved or TooManyRefusals, and a start PathFollowing refuses fails NotFinite or Singular (path_cause); the engine record holds PathFollowing's StepFailure. The endpoint passes verified_landing.

GameTracing.fisher_rao_spread — Method

A player's move from from to to, its logits in range, and its logits' spread at to. The move is the Fisher–Rao distance 4 asin(h / 2), h the Euclidean distance between the square roots of the two strategies: a geodesic on the probability simplex, at most π. A small move of logits Δz has length √Var_π(Δz). The spread is the root mean square √(Σ π z²) of the logits z = λ w[range] at to under their own mixed strategy π.

GameTracing.verified_landing — Method

The TraceResult at tracer's point on its target precision, after one more Newton correction there with λ pinned (pinned_correction), the check of BifurcationKit's verified_endpoint. That correction fails NotFinite or Singular (AtEndpoint). It must settle under the trace's own corrector (settle_test) and in logits (endpoint_check, a LogitSettled), else it fails PrecisionExhausted (AtEndpoint). Its StalledCorrection holds the correction's logit move (logit_change) and its iterate, pinned at the endpoint's precision, as BifurcationKit's does. Else the point is Ok(Traced), its logit_accuracy that logit move. The logit test bounds the endpoint's logits where the default StrategyDistance does not: a strategy off the support counts by its probability.

GameTracing.gambit_settings — Method
gambit_settings(E, max_accel)

BifurcationKitSettings in E for pygambit's max_accel, which caps each step's growth (StepControl's max_step_growth, and aggressiveness = max_accel - 1). BifurcationKit's growth limit must exceed 1, so at max_accel = 1 step control only refuses steps. Zero aggressiveness then holds an accepted step's length, where Gambit also shrinks it after a poor corrector. The first step comes from the game (opening_step), as in PathFollowingSettings. The trace steps in default_step_metric, which PathFollowingSettings shares.
