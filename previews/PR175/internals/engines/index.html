Internals: engines

The PathFollowing engine and pygambit's logit_solve interface.

GameTracing.LogProbabilitySettled — Type
LogProbabilitySettled(ranges, tolerance, rounding)

PathFollowing's converged over points [w; λ], each player's logits in its range of ranges (logit_ranges). The last correction moves the profile at most tolerance in Gambit's norm, past rounding times each strategy's rounding scale (log_probability_corrections). Tuple ranges make it isbits.

GameTracing.PositivePrecision — Type
PositivePrecision(accept_corrected)

PathFollowing's accept: it refuses a corrected point at precision zero or below, and one that accept_corrected refuses (corrected_accepted, converged true). The principal branch meets precision zero only at its start, so such a point went back along it; the refusal retries a shorter step. AcceptAll() refuses no other point, and builds no logits.

GameTracing.default_split_check — Method

PathFollowingSettings' default split check: trigger 1.3 and tolerance 10 times distance's settle tolerance. At growth 1.1 a step held by a step-control target rides at f near 1. So trigger 1 fired on up to 67% of logitnashspeed's steps and cost up to 33% per game (median). Trigger 1.3 fired on 0.4-2.7% of them and cost at most 2%. A hunt over 20,000 two-action games and 1200 unfolded pitchforks found the same 7 jumps in Float32 and Float64. Trigger 1.3 fired on and refused each; the smallest jump f was 1.41. At growth 2 with the summed metric, jumps came as low as f 1.1: a looser step control needs trigger 1. A zero settle tolerance gives the tolerance no scale, so it throws an ArgumentError that asks for an explicit `splitcheck`.

GameTracing.engine_endpoint — Method
engine_endpoint(game, start, pass_work, settings::PathFollowingSettings, callbacks, context)

trace_endpoint on PathFollowing from the finite start = [w; 0] (trace_start) on game's pass work pass_work, to a nonzero target: one trace whose steps stop there (stepped_landing!!), with callbacks = (; keep_going, accept_corrected, redraw). accept_corrected sees converged points only (converged is true), and its false refuses the point, which shrinks the step (PositivePrecision). A stop short of the target fails TraceStuck for PathFollowing's Unresolved, NoConvergence for a spent budget, and a start PathFollowing refuses fails NotFinite or Singular (path_cause); the engine record holds PathFollowing's StepFailure. The endpoint passes verified_landing.

GameTracing.path_problem — Method

PathFollowing's CurveProblem of the principal branch of pass!!'s game (LogitGame) in [w; λ]: from the flat start (trace_start) toward rising λ, in the storage of flat, one zero per logit. GameTracing.stepped_landing!! — Method

tracer advanced to precision, and the point of its branch there: its steps stop there (path_step!!), at the first crossing a predictor or a corrected point shows. keep_going(logits, λ) runs at each accepted point, and false short of precision fails the trace TraceStuck (StoppedShort). context rides on every failure. Use only the returned tracer.

source
GameTracing.verified_landing — Method

The TraceResult at tracer's point on its target precision, after one more Newton correction there with λ pinned (pinned_correction), the check of BifurcationKit's verified_endpoint. That correction fails NotFinite or Singular (AtEndpoint). It must settle under the trace's own corrector (settle_test) and in Gambit's norm, at most tolerance past ulps ulps of each strategy's rounding scale (LogProbabilitySettled), else it fails PrecisionExhausted (AtEndpoint). Its StalledCorrection holds the correction's move (LogProbabilityChange) and its iterate, pinned at the endpoint's precision, as BifurcationKit's does. Else the point is Ok(Traced), its logit_accuracy that move.

source
GameTracing.DEFAULT_STALL_WINDOW — Constant

logit_solve's default stall_window (regret_stalled). On 450 games (GAMUT, maxregret_reach, the fold set) in Float32 and Float64 on both engines, traced past the default maxregret with no cap, a trace that settled ran at most 100 rising points with no new least regret before it settled; 200 is twice that. At the floor, regret takes a few rounded values: of 152 traces run to 20,000 points whose least regret did not halve over their second half, the window ended 87. The others still set a new least regret every few dozen points, as λ creeps up.

GameTracing.BranchRecorder — Type
BranchRecorder(gains, tolerance, window)

A keep_going that appends each accepted point of a trace of gains to precisions and logits, its largest regret (max_regret!) to regrets and the least regret so far to least. It stops the trace at the first point whose regret is at most tolerance, or where the regret stalls at its rounding floor over window points (regret_stalled).

GameTracing.branch_outcome — Method

The points of recorder once its trace ended (engine_points, Ok(nothing) or the engine's failure), with context, checked the same on both engines. A settled last point returns every point. A regret stall fails PrecisionExhausted (regret_stall); so does a trace that reached precision_ceiling unsettled. Else the engine's failure stands.

GameTracing.gambit_settings — Method
gambit_settings(E, max_accel)

BifurcationKitSettings in E for pygambit's max_accel, which caps each step's growth (StepControl's max_step_growth, and aggressiveness = max_accel - 1). BifurcationKit's growth limit must exceed 1, so at max_accel = 1 step control only refuses steps. Zero aggressiveness then holds an accepted step's length, where Gambit also shrinks it after a poor corrector. The first step comes from the game (opening_step), as in PathFollowingSettings. The trace steps in default_step_metric, which PathFollowingSettings shares.

GameTracing.precision_ceiling — Method
precision_ceiling(E)

The target precision of every logit_solve trace: eps(E)^-2, 2^46 in Float32 and 2^104 in Float64. It makes the target finite. On every measured game the regret stalls before it (regret_stalled). Its square must be finite, so a pass that squares the precision stays finite (ArgumentError otherwise, as in Float16). logit_solve_lambda has its own targets.

GameTracing.regret_stall — Method

The cause of a regret stall at recorder's last point [w; λ]: PrecisionExhausted (AtEndpoint), its StalledCorrection the endpoint check's Newton correction there with λ pinned (pinned_correction), its move in Gambit's norm with no rounding allowance. A correction that fails gives its own cause (NotFinite, Singular).

source
GameTracing.regret_stalled — Method

Whether recorder's regret stalled at its rounding floor: its last window points each rose in precision and none set a new least regret. Near an equilibrium the regret falls as λ rises, like 1 / λ at a mixed one, so a regret that holds over the window has met the float type's rounding. A fold, where λ falls, restarts the window.

source
